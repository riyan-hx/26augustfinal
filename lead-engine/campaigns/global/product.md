# What I do

I am Riyan Safeekh — a product designer and founder based in Kozhikode, Kerala.
Portfolio: https://riyan.lumid.in

I design product UI for early-stage software teams, and I hand it over dev-ready.
I am not a visual decorator and I do not do logos, brochures or social media
creatives. What I do is take a product that already has users or a working
backend and make the screens people actually move through — onboarding, the main
workspace, dashboards, settings, empty and error states — clear enough that
people finish what they started.

## The offer I lead with

**A one-week Product UI Sprint — fixed scope, fixed price.**

- Day 1: a short call, then I map the flow you actually care about and where it
  is leaking users.
- Days 2-4: I design that flow properly in Figma — real states, real data,
  mobile and desktop, not a pretty hero shot.
- Day 5: handover — a Figma file with components and tokens your developer can
  build from directly, plus a short walkthrough of why each decision is there.

Fixed price, $1,800 for the sprint. If the first flow works, teams usually
continue flow by flow at the same rate. No retainer, no lock-in, no agency
minimum, no discovery phase that bills for three weeks before anything is drawn.

I am a founder, not a freelancer looking for hours. I am building **Lumid AI**, a
practice-management platform for independent psychologists in India — booking,
payment before session, patient records and follow-ups. I designed it and I built
the front end myself, in React. It is a working prototype, pre-revenue, and I am
in the middle of five weeks of customer interviews through TinkerHub's Nights &
Weekends programme to validate it before building further. Lumid AI was also
selected for the KPH Hackathon in Kochi.

That matters to a founder for one practical reason: I have had to make the same
trade-offs they are making. I know what it costs to ship a screen, because I
ship them. I read a product as a business decision first and a layout second,
and I hand over files a developer can build from without a translation meeting,
because I have been the developer on the other side of that handover.
