Scope: Lumid AI public marketing website (lumid-site/: index, features, pricing, about, contact). Mode: Persuade.

Audience: independent Indian psychologists who run their practice on WhatsApp/UPI. Job: understand that Lumid confirms a session only after payment, see the real dashboard, start free. Proof: the real dashboard screens (from Figma v3, General Sans compact), honest early-stage framing, no testimonials, logos or traction numbers. Pricing: free to start plus a small % per paid booking. The exact % is TBD and shown as TBD.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A booking only counts once it says CNF. The site borrows the Indian Railways reservation chart (PNR codes, WL→CNF status, ruled chart tables, perforated ticket stubs) to make payment-before-confirmation instantly familiar. It refuses the centred-headline-plus-screenshot SaaS page.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks on paper: forest #0f2a17 and lime #8bd65e on sage paper #eef2ec with white stubs. Ruled 1px chart tables, perforated stub edges, booking codes in mono (data only), tabular figures, General Sans. Pill controls as in the dashboard. Status is shown by stamp form (WL outline, CNF filled lime), never a third hue.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own leak (unpaid holds, no-shows, chasing on WhatsApp), sees the mechanism (WL until paid, then CNF), browses the real dashboard as a chart, trusts the consent stance, and starts free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left, the headline "Booked. Paid. Confirmed." at about 7vw, with the subline and Start free plus "See how it works" under it. On the right, a white perforated ticket stub (code, patient, session, fee) whose status rolls from WL 1 to CNF when "₹1,500 received" lands. Below the fold edge, a ruled chart band leads into the overview screen. Start free also sits in the nav.

FORM: The Reservation Chart, #7 of the grounded list (seed key fb97082c). Raises: master 12-col module (Swiss), tabular digits that roll (Nixie), real session durations on every row (J-card), two-ink commitment (Ikeda). Signature interaction: the hero stub's WL→CNF confirmation, plus a features chart whose rows switch the screen preview.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
