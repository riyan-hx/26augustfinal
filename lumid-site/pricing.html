Pricing questions

Want to talk it through? Contact us.

Why a per-booking fee instead of a subscription?

A solo practice has quiet months. A per-booking fee means Lumid costs nothing when you aren’t seeing patients and only earns when you’re paid.

When will the rate be decided?

During early access, with the psychologists using Lumid. We’ll publish it here before anyone is charged.

Do I pay anything for cancelled sessions?

No. Unpaid, expired and cancelled bookings carry no Lumid fee.

Who sets my session fees?

You do. Each session type can have its own length and fee, for example ₹1,500 for 50 minutes or ₹2,000 for a first consultation.
