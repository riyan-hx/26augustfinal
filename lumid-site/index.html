Questions

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

What if a patient can’t pay right away?

The slot stays held for a short window while they pay. If the payment doesn’t come through, the slot is released and goes back on your page. You can also add a booking yourself, for example for a patient who pays in person.

Do my patients need to install anything?

No. They open your booking link, pick a time and pay in the browser. The Lumid app for check-ins between sessions is optional.

Can Lumid see my session notes?

Your notes are part of your practice record and are not used for anything else. Check-ins from the patient app are visible to you only when the patient chooses to share them.

I already use WhatsApp and UPI. Why switch?

Keep using them. Share your Lumid link on WhatsApp, and patients still pay with UPI. What changes is that the booking, the payment and the reminder now happen in one place without you.

Is Lumid available outside India?

Lumid is built for Indian practices first, with fees in rupees and Indian payment methods. Tell us if you practise elsewhere and we’ll let you know when that changes.
