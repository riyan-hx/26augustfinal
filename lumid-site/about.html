Care deserves fewer loose ends.

Lumid is a small team in Kozhikode, Kerala, building infrastructure for independent mental-health practice in India.

Photo placeholderFounder portrait, 4:5. Replace with your image.

Most psychologists in India run their practice alone, with no receptionist, no clinic software and no one to chase a payment.

Bookings arrive on WhatsApp. Payments arrive as UPI screenshots. Follow-ups live in memory. Every one of those handoffs is a place where a session slips, an hour goes unpaid, or a patient who needed a check-in doesn’t get one.

Lumid started from one rule: a session isn’t confirmed until it’s paid. Everything else, from the calendar and patient records to follow-ups and earnings, is built around that rule, so the practice runs without the admin.

Lumid is founded by Riyan Safeekh, a computer science student in Kozhikode with a background in product design and security.
