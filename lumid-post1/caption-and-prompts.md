# Lumid first post

## Caption

Your session ends at 7. Your practice keeps going till 11.

Rescheduling on WhatsApp. Checking UPI screenshots. Trying to remember where you left off with a client you saw last Tuesday.

Lumid puts that part of your day in one place: bookings, payment before the session, and follow-ups. We're building it in Kozhikode, and it's early. There's a working prototype and no paying users yet.

If you run an independent practice and want to try it first, comment PRACTICE and we'll DM you.

#psychologistkerala #privatepractice #therapistsofindia

## Swapping in the photos

Save the images as `cover-bg.jpg` and `cta-bg.jpg` in the `source` folder, then run:

    python render.py .

from inside `source`. New PNGs land in `source/out`. The cover has a soft top shade so the white headline stays readable on bright skies.

Before posting, check that the prototype flows on slides 4-6 still work as shown. They are labelled "Prototype · demo data".
