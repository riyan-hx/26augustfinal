You do the therapy. Lumid does the admin.

Your last client leaves at 7. Then the second shift starts: moving Tuesday's 5 PM client to Thursday over WhatsApp, checking whether a ₹1,500 UPI payment came in, and sending the reminder you meant to send yesterday.

Lumid is a dashboard that takes that shift off your evening. A client picks a free slot, pays before the session and gets a reminder, without you typing a single message. You open one screen and see your week.

We're building it in Kozhikode with psychologists, and it's early. What you see here is our working prototype with demo data.
