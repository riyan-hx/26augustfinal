Introducing Lumid, the operating system for independent practice.

Your last client leaves at 7. Then the second shift starts: moving Tuesday's 5 PM client to Thursday over WhatsApp, checking whether a ₹1,500 UPI payment came in, and sending the reminder you meant to send yesterday.

Lumid puts that shift in one dashboard. Clients book and pay through one link, the reminder goes out on its own, and you see your day, your payments and your follow-ups on a single screen.

We're building it in Kozhikode with psychologists, and it's early. Every number on that screen is demo data from our working prototype.
