@lumid.ai went live on Instagram today, with its first post. It's a teaser, and the dashboard in it is blurred on purpose.

Here's why it exists.

Many independent psychologists here in Kerala, from Kozhikode to Kochi, run their whole practice from WhatsApp and UPI. Sessions end around 7. Then a second shift starts: reschedule messages, checking a ₹1,500 payment screenshot, a reminder that should have gone out at 10 PM. None of that is therapy, and it eats the evening.

That second shift is what I'm building Lumid for. One dashboard for bookings, payment before the session and follow-ups.

Where it stands right now:
A working prototype. Every name and number on that screen is demo data.
No paying users yet.
I'm still interviewing psychologists through TinkerHub Nights & Weekends to check I've got the problem right before I build more.

We started the Instagram from zero because that's where a lot of these psychologists already show up for their clients. Building for them somewhere they don't spend time felt wrong.

If you run your own practice as a psychologist or counsellor, or you know someone who does, I'd like 20 minutes with you. Tell me where your evenings actually go. Comment or DM me and I'll reach out.
