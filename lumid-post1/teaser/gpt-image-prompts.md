# Lumid teaser: prompts for ChatGPT image generation

How to use: attach your current teaser design (the "Something BIG is coming" one) to ChatGPT, then paste the **base prompt** followed by **one** variation block. Generate one variation per chat message. If it misspells any text, reply: "Same image, fix the text to read exactly: ..." and paste the correct line.

---

## Base prompt (paste first, every time)

Use the attached image as the layout and style reference. Recreate it as a polished Instagram portrait post, 1080 x 1350 pixels (4:5), with the same overall composition: brand row at the top, a large three-line headline on the upper left, a tilted product dashboard card coming in from the right and bleeding off the right edge, and a footer row at the bottom.

Brand and style rules:
- Brand name in the top-left is the wordmark "LUMID AI" in bold, geometric, uppercase sans-serif, in green (#2f8a1a). Do not invent a logo symbol, sparkle, star or icon.
- Top-right small text: "For independent psychologists", in a plain medium-weight sans-serif, muted grey-green (#5f6a5b), sentence case, normal letter spacing. Not all caps and not widely spaced.
- Typeface for all text: a clean modern grotesk similar to General Sans or Helvetica Now. No serif, no monospace, no handwriting.
- The one huge word in the headline is heavy (bold or black weight), uppercase, tight letter spacing, filled with a vertical green gradient from deep forest green (#0a4a23) at the top to bright green (#43e07a) at the bottom. The lines above and below it are regular weight, dark forest (#242E21), sentence case.
- Background: soft off-white paper (#F6F8F2) with a very faint square grid and fine film grain. Behind the dashboard, soft vertical bars of green light (like light through blinds), blurred and fading out, concentrated around the dashboard, not in the empty corners.
- The dashboard: keep the dashboard from the reference image, but tilt it about 10 degrees, give it a soft white frame and a long, soft green-tinted drop shadow, and **blur its contents strongly so that no numbers, names or words on it can be read**. Add a small white pill tag on its top-left edge that reads "Sneak peek" with a small lime-green dot (#88D95F) before the text.
- Footer: bottom-left small muted text "@lumid.ai · Kozhikode". Bottom-right, a dark forest-green (#242E21) rounded pill button with text: "Comment PRACTICE for early access →", where only the word PRACTICE is lime green (#88D95F) and the rest is off-white.
- Generous margins (about 76 px left and right). Calm, premium, editorial software-launch feel, like a funded SaaS company's announcement post.

Strict rules:
- Render every piece of text exactly as written, with correct spelling, including "psychologists". Add no other text anywhere.
- No emoji, no sparkles, no purple, no gradients on any text except the one huge headline word, no glowing orbit lines, no stock photos of people, no mockup hands.
- No readable fake statistics, percentages or rupee amounts anywhere.

## Fix-up prompts (if the first result is off)

- Text wrong: "Keep everything identical, but change the text to read exactly: [paste line]."
- Dashboard readable: "Keep everything identical, but blur the dashboard contents much more, so no text or numbers can be read."
- Too busy: "Keep the layout, but remove anything that is not listed in my prompt and add more empty space around the headline."
- Wrong brand colour: "Use only these colours: #F6F8F2, #242E21, #2f8a1a, #88D95F, and the green gradient on the big word."
